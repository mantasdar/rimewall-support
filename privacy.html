Rimewall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Rimewall does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you.

What stays on your device

Your progress (medals, best wave, upgrades and the stand in progress) and your sound setting are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network

The game runs entirely offline. It makes no network requests.

Children

Rimewall collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

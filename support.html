Rimewall Support

Need help or found a bug? Email game@tafe.lt and say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Common questions

How do I move? Tap the ground and the keeper walks there, or drag a finger anywhere to steer and lift to stop.

There is no sound. Check the Sound button at the bottom of the game screen and your volume. Sound plays even when the silent switch is on.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved only on your device and is removed if the app is deleted.
